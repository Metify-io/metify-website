According to a recent IDC survey, the enterprise market is roughly split 50/50 between running containers on-premises versus in the cloud. MOJO makes deployment seamless regardless of your preferred approach.

Container orchestration platforms like Kubernetes, Red Hat OpenShift, and Google Anthos have transformed how enterprises deploy and manage applications. But the underlying infrastructure — whether bare metal servers or virtual machines — still needs to be provisioned, configured, and maintained.

MOJO Platform bridges this gap by automating the entire bare metal lifecycle: from initial discovery and BIOS configuration, through OS installation and Kubernetes node preparation, to ongoing firmware management and compliance monitoring.

Whether you're running containers directly on bare metal for maximum performance, or provisioning VMs that host your container workloads, MOJO provides a single pane of glass for managing your physical infrastructure.
